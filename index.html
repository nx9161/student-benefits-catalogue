Graduation Freebies — Class of 2026

verified programs only · year-specific · check before you go

These are not the year-round student discounts on the catalogue page. They’re short, graduation-season treats — mostly “show up in cap & gown on one day” or “a local franchise chooses to be generous.” Dates, participating locations, and stock change every year, so treat every card as verify before you rely on it.

How to use this page (read this first)

1) In-store treats: go on the exact date, in cap & gown or with diploma / student ID, to a participating location only. 2) Local-only treats (Chick-fil-A, Golden Corral): there is no national program — check your store’s Facebook / Instagram or call ahead. 3) Mail-an-announcement trend: you physically mail a graduation announcement to a brand HQ. Some students report surprise gifts (LEGO, Sun Bum, Crumbl and others go viral most years) — responses are NOT guaranteed, most brands never reply, and postage is on you. Only mail brands you actually like, and never send original documents.

Honest note on Nike & Adidas

  • Nike and Adidas do NOT run “free stuff if you email us that you’re graduating” programs. Nike has a 10% student discount (SheerID) and a “gifts for graduation” shopping guide; Adidas has a student discount via UNiDAYS. Those are discounts on the Catalogue page — not graduation freebies, and emailing them will not mail you free shoes.
  • No brand on this page has promised to email/mail you a freebie just because you wrote from a .edu address. Where outreach helps (asking a local store, or mailing an announcement), the template says so plainly.

Mail your graduation announcement to brands — the viral trend

Every spring, graduates mail a printed graduation announcement (the card you’d send family) to favourite brands’ HQ / consumer-relations addresses. A few brands sometimes mail back samples, coupons, or a congrats gift — most send nothing. It’s a fun, low-odds tradition, not a program. Budget your own envelopes + stamps, send only copies, and don’t expect a reply before graduation day.
