How claiming actually works — the 4 steps almost every offer follows

  • 1. Verify once. Create your free account on the platform the offer uses (UNiDAYS, Student Beans, SheerID, or ID.me) and prove you’re a student — school email first; if the automatic check fails, upload an enrollment document (student ID, class schedule, or tuition receipt).
  • 2. Activate from the Apply button. Every card’s Apply button opens the exact page where that offer is claimed. Start there so your verified status attaches to the purchase or plan.
  • 3. Confirm before you pay. Check the student price, discount line, or plan name on the final checkout screen — that screen, not a blog post, is the truth. Each card’s “How to claim — start to finish” steps tell you what you should see.
  • 4. Re-verify on schedule. Most platforms re-check your student status about once a year (the GitHub Pack about every 2 years). Re-verify promptly and the benefit keeps running; let it lapse and standard pricing returns.

Which platform do I need?

  • UNiDAYS — a free account you verify once, then shop through its links/codes. Powers Apple Education, adidas, ASOS, UNIQLO, Max and more. All of them live on the UNiDAYS page.
  • SheerID — not a shopping account; it’s the verification check that pops up inside a brand’s own signup (Spotify, Hulu, Adobe, DoorDash, YouTube Premium…). Those offers live on the SheerID page.
  • Student Beans — like UNiDAYS: verify once, get a fresh code per order (H&M, Urban Outfitters, Lululemon…).
  • ID.me — verify once, then use the ID.me button at checkout (Samsung, Lenovo, Skechers, Verizon, movie tickets…).
  • ISIC — the International Student Identity Card for travel, hostels, museums and transport outside the US.

If a verification fails

  • Use your full legal name exactly as your school has it — nicknames are the #1 cause of failed automatic checks.
  • Upload a document that shows your name, your school, and a current term: class schedule or tuition receipt works best; a student ID alone sometimes doesn’t show dates.
  • Reviews can take from a few minutes to a couple of days. Finish the brand signup in the same session once you’re approved so the student price attaches.
  • Still stuck? The brand’s student-support page (linked from each Apply button) is the right place — this catalogue can’t verify you itself.

Mail your graduation announcement to brands — the viral trend

Every spring, graduates mail a printed graduation announcement (the card you’d send family) to favourite brands’ HQ / consumer-relations addresses. A few brands sometimes mail back samples, coupons, or a congrats gift — most send nothing. It’s a fun, low-odds tradition, not a program. Budget your own envelopes + stamps, send only copies, and don’t expect a reply before graduation day.
